# 8 Ball Pool Hack 2026 - Precision Diamond

A difficult bank shot, an awkward cue ball position or a small aiming mistake can cost you an entire game of pool.

Precision Diamond is a free billiards aim trainer project inspired by 8 Ball Pool. It focuses on shot geometry, extended aiming guidelines, cushion rebounds and cue ball positioning.

Instead of guessing where the ball will travel, study the trajectory, understand the angle and practice your next shot with greater precision.

## 📐 Bank Shot & Cushion Prediction

Bank shots are one of the most satisfying parts of billiards. They are also among the easiest shots to miscalculate.

Precision Diamond includes a dedicated trajectory design for studying cushion rebounds.

| Shot Type | Practice Focus |
|:---|:---|
| Direct Shot | Straight pocket alignment |
| Bank Shot | Object ball cushion rebound |
| Kick Shot | Cue ball cushion rebound |
| Double Bank | Two-cushion trajectories |
| Multi-Rail Shot | Multiple cushion rebounds |
| Combination Shot | Multiple-ball contact |
| Thin Cut | Difficult contact angles |

Experiment with different angles and understand how cushion contact changes the direction of a ball.
